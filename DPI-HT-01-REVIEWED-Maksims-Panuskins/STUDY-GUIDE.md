## Changed-fact method

For any changed fact, answer in this order:

1. Does the accounting classification change?
2. What changes in profit?
3. What changes in cash?
4. What asset or liability changes?
5. Does equity change through profit or distribution?

Example: if the September work had been delivered and accepted on 31 August, revenue and profit would increase by €90k, the contract liability would decrease by €90k, cash would not change, and equity would increase by €90k.
